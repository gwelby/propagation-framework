## 5. Why the Identity Jump Fails

Even if Fisher metrics appear in multiple places, that does not prove identity.

At most, repeated use can support:

- analogy,
- structural family resemblance,
- reusable mathematical language,
- or a promising unification program.

It does **not** prove:

\[
\text{Axiom 3} \equiv \text{Fisher Information}.
\]

To prove identity, one would need:

1. a precise PF definition of Axiom 3 in mathematical form,
2. a proof that Fisher Information is necessary and sufficient for that form,
3. consistency across the physics, cognition, and operational layers without false imports.

None of that is currently present.
