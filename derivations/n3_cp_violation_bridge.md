## The Mathematical Basis

### CKM Matrix for N Generations

The CKM matrix is an N×N unitary matrix. For quark mixing, it parameterizes the mismatch between the mass eigenstates and weak interaction eigenstates.

**Counting parameters:**
- An N×N unitary matrix has N² real parameters
- Quark field phase redefinitions can absorb (2N-1) phases (one overall phase is unobservable)
- Remaining: N² - (2N-1) = (N-1)² parameters
- Of these: N(N-1)/2 are rotation angles, (N-1)(N-2)/2 are complex phases

| Generations | Rotation Angles | Complex Phases | CP Violation Possible? |
|-------------|-----------------|----------------|----------------------|
| N=1 | 0 | 0 | No |
| N=2 | 1 | 0 | **No** |
| N=3 | 3 | 1 | **Yes** |
| N=4 | 6 | 3 | Yes |

**The key result:** N=3 is the **structural minimum** for CP violation through quark mixing. For N=2, all complex phases can be rotated away by quark field redefinitions. The first physical CP-violating phase appears at N=3.

This is standard particle physics (Kobayashi & Maskawa, 1973 — Nobel Prize 2008). The 3-generation prediction was made BEFORE the third generation was discovered.

## What This Does NOT Claim

1. **It does NOT derive the CP phase magnitude.** The CKM phase δ ≈ 1.2 rad (≈ 69°) is measured, not derived. PF has no account of its specific value.

2. **It does NOT derive the full CKM mixing angles.** The three rotation angles (θ₁₂, θ₂₃, θ₁₃) are measured, not derived.

3. **It does NOT address CP violation in the lepton sector.** The PMNS matrix for neutrinos also contains a CP-violating phase (δ_CP). If neutrinos are Majorana, there are additional phases. This bridge addresses quarks only.

4. **It does NOT explain baryogenesis.** CP violation is a NECESSARY condition for matter/antimatter asymmetry (Sakharov condition #2), but it is not SUFFICIENT. The SM's CP violation is too weak by ~10 orders of magnitude. PF would need additional CP sources or a different baryogenesis mechanism.

5. **It does NOT claim the phase value follows from PF axioms.** The bridge is structural (N=3 → phase exists), not quantitative (N=3 → phase = X).
