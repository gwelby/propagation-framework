## 1. The statement we can defend

The Propagation Framework currently separates the Koide problem into two layers:

1. **Amplitude geometry**: the charged-lepton Koide condition `Q = 2/3`, equivalently
   `R/A = sqrt(2)`, is derived as the equilateral / equal-amplitude geometry of the charged-lepton
   sector.
2. **Phase orientation**: the reduced Koide phase
   `delta_0 mod 2pi/3 ~= 0.222229631490 rad ~= 2/9`
   is a strong empirical anchor, but the framework does not yet derive the selector that fixes this
   orientation.

That is the clean public line.

---

## 2. What changed on 2026-04-20

The team had been blurring two different questions:

- **harmonic-purity question**: can a mechanism isolate `cos(9 delta)`?
- **phase-selection question**: can a mechanism select the observed charged-lepton phase
  `delta ~= 2/9`?

The 2026-04-20 tolerance audit showed these are not equivalent.

The exact Chebyshev cubic isolates the `9 delta` harmonic, but its minima are not near the empirical
charged-lepton phase. Therefore:

> producing `cos(9 delta)` is not the same thing as deriving `delta ~= 2/9`.

This distinction must govern all public language from here forward.

## 6. Manuscript-safe paragraph

The charged-lepton Koide ratio and the charged-lepton Koide phase now have different evidentiary
statuses. The ratio `Q = 2/3` is the geometry the framework claims to derive. The phase
`delta ~= 2/9` remains an empirical anchor. We audited several plausible selector mechanisms after
Rivero's challenge, including projective slope variables, the exact `cos(9 delta)` Chebyshev cubic,
character decomposition, and the historical proxy potential. Each failed to produce the observed
phase. The honest conclusion is therefore narrower and stronger: the amplitude geometry survives;
the phase selector is still open.
