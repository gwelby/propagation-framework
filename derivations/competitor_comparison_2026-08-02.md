### What rivals have that PF doesn't:
- **Forward predictions.** UGP has 5+ (neutrino mass ratio, dark sector particle, δ_CP, r=0, w=−1). PF has zero (PRED-001 BLOCKED, PRED-002 HOLD).

### The direct competition:

**PF vs UGP on α:** UGP claims derived; PF says open. If UGP is right, PF should be able to derive it too. If PF's look-elsewhere critique is right, UGP's "exact 137" may be an artifact. This is a genuine disagreement that could be resolved by analysis.

**PF vs UGP on forward predictions:** UGP wins decisively. PF has none.
