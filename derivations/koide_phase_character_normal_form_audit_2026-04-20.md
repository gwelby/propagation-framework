## 2. Setup

Let

`x = (x_0, x_1, x_2) in R^3`

be any real triple.

For the bounded algebraic test, let `P` be the cyclic permutation

`P(x_0, x_1, x_2) = (x_1, x_2, x_0)`.

This is a mathematical choice of labeling, not a PF-derived physical action on the charged leptons.

Then `R^3` decomposes under the cyclic action as

`R^3 = R * (1,1,1) (+) V_0`

where

`V_0 = { (y_0, y_1, y_2) : y_0 + y_1 + y_2 = 0 }`

is the faithful `2D` real representation.

Write

`A = (x_0 + x_1 + x_2)/3`

and

`y = x - A * (1,1,1) in V_0`.

---

## 3. Explicit faithful basis

Choose the standard basis of `V_0`

`u = (1, -1/2, -1/2)`

`v = (0, -sqrt(3)/2, sqrt(3)/2)`.

Then

`y = alpha * u + beta * v`

with

`alpha = (2*x_0 - x_1 - x_2)/3`

`beta  = (x_2 - x_1)/sqrt(3)`.

These are exactly the canonical coordinates used in the projective pass.

---

## 4. The `Z_3` action on the faithful piece

Under the cyclic permutation `P`,

`P u = (-1/2) u - (sqrt(3)/2) v`

`P v = (sqrt(3)/2) u - (1/2) v`.

So on the coefficient pair `(alpha, beta)`, the generator acts by the matrix

`R_{-120} = [[-1/2,  sqrt(3)/2], [-sqrt(3)/2, -1/2]]`.

This is exactly a rotation by `-2*pi/3`.

### Consequence

The faithful real `2D` piece already carries the standard `Z_3` rotation structure.

No charged-lepton-specific input has appeared yet.

---

## 5. Natural complex structure

Define the complex coordinate

`z = alpha + i beta`.

Then the cyclic generator acts as

`z -> omega^2 z`

with

`omega = exp(2*pi*i/3)`.

If one reverses orientation and uses `z = alpha - i beta`, the action is by `omega` instead.

### Lemma

The `P`-compatible complex structure on `V_0` is unique up to sign.

### Reason

The centralizer of a nontrivial planar rotation inside `End_R(V_0)` is exactly the real algebra
generated by `I` and the quarter-turn `J`.

So any commuting complex structure must be one of the two choices `+J` or `-J`.

Therefore the complex coordinate on the faithful piece is canonical up to orientation reversal.

---

## 6. Exact normal form

Using the basis above,

`y_0 = alpha`

`y_1 = -alpha/2 - (sqrt(3)/2) beta`

`y_2 = -alpha/2 + (sqrt(3)/2) beta`.

But these are exactly the real parts of the `Z_3` orbit:

`y_k = Re(omega^k z)`,   `k = 0,1,2`

after the basis convention fixed above.

Therefore:

`x_k = A + Re(omega^k z)`.

This is the exact representation-theoretic normal form of a real triple under the cyclic action.

---

## 7. Recovery of the Koide parametrization

Write

`z = R * exp(i delta)`.

Then

`x_k = A + R * cos(delta + 2*pi*k/3)`.

This is exactly the Koide-type parametrization.

If one further imposes the Koide amplitude condition

`Q = 2/3`

then the earlier geometry gives

`R/A = sqrt(2)`,

so the form becomes

`x_k = A * (1 + sqrt(2) * cos(delta + 2*pi*k/3))`.

### Verdict of this step

The `1 + 2` / character decomposition does **not** derive something deeper than the Koide form.

It recovers the Koide form exactly.

---

## 8. Link to the projective lemma

The phase coordinate obtained here is not new.

From the definitions above,

`tan(delta) = beta / alpha`

which is exactly

`tan(delta) = ((x_2 - x_1)/sqrt(3)) / ((2*x_0 - x_1 - x_2)/3)`.

This is the same canonical projective coordinate fenced in
[koide_projective_mobius_lemma_2026-04-20.md](/mnt/d/Fundamentals/derivations/koide_projective_mobius_lemma_2026-04-20.md).

So the character-decomposition lane collapses onto the already-classified projective lane once the
faithful `2D` piece is complexified.

## 9. Why this is a no-go for the bridge

This decomposition is mathematically real, but it is too general.

It applies to **every** labeled real triple.

Therefore it does not explain:

1. why the charged-lepton triple specifically should be regarded as a physical `Z_3` orbit,
2. why the phase `delta` should be selected dynamically,
3. why the ordering / orientation should be physically canonical,
4. why the Koide amplitude condition `R/A = sqrt(2)` should hold.

It only says:

> once you have any real triple, its traceless part is a 2D real vector; once you complexify that
> 2D plane, the cyclic permutation acts by cube roots of unity and the triple takes Koide normal
> form.

That is a representation-theoretic repackaging, not a selector.
