## 1. The Plain-Language Definition
The Medium is not an invisible fluid filling up empty space. The Medium *is* the capacity for space, time, and matter to exist. 

It is the underlying structural rulebook of reality. It dictates exactly how a change happening *here* is allowed to coherently affect something over *there*, and it enforces a strict maximum speed limit on how fast that influence can travel. It is not the canvas that the painting is drawn on; it is the mathematical tension of the canvas itself.

## 2. The "Smallest Dot" Explanation
Humans are wired to look for the "smallest dot." When we look at a table, we see wood. We look closer and see molecules, then atoms, then protons, then quarks. We keep zooming in, expecting that at the very bottom of the universe, we will finally find a tiny, solid, indestructible pebble of *stuff*.

But there are no pebbles. 

If you zoom in to the absolute smallest possible dot, you do not find a noun. You find a verb. You find an event. 

The "smallest dot" is simply the smallest distinguishable change that the rules of reality allow to propagate. What we call "matter" (the solid pebble) is just what it looks like when a change gets trapped in a stable, repeating, resonant loop. You are never looking at a solid object; you are looking at a beautifully sustained echo in the Medium.

## 3. Danger Words to Avoid (The Soma-Check)
To protect the framework from being dismissed by physicists or co-opted by mystics, the canonical definition must explicitly avoid these traps:

*   **Aether / Ether:** *Why it's dangerous:* Implies a static, physical fluid with a "preferred rest frame," violating Einstein's Relativity. The Medium has no preferred frame; it generates relativistic limits.
*   **Faster-Than-Light / Instantaneous signaling:** *Why it's dangerous:* Violates Axiom 2. Entanglement is not a faster-than-light text message between two particles; it is two measurements of the *same* extended, coherent wave.
*   **Building Blocks / Legos:** *Why it's dangerous:* Forces the mind back into "pebble" ontology. Reality is built of constraints and resonance, not bricks.
*   **Pure Energy:** *Why it's dangerous:* Energy is not a glowing substance. In physics, energy is a measurement of frequency ($E=hf$). To say the medium is "pure energy" is to say it is "pure frequency" without defining what is oscillating.
