## 1. Minimal Definition

**The Medium is the minimal causal substrate — the structure that:**
1. Defines the partition between what can and cannot causally affect what (causal cones)
2. Supports the propagation of distinguishable changes (waves, modes, fields)
3. Allows stable self-reinforcing patterns (particles, coherent structures)
4. Generates measurable gradients that appear as forces, curvature, refraction

It is not: a substance, an ether, a container, a background.
It is: the lawful constraint-structure that makes propagation possible.

**The tightest version:**

> The Medium is what makes finite-speed causal propagation possible — not an extra ingredient, but the minimal structure without which propagation, coherence, and causality cannot be defined.

**What it is not:**
- Not an ether (which requires a preferred frame — falsified by SR/Michelson-Morley)
- Not a container (which would require something outside it)
- Not a substance (which would require asking what it is made of, recursively)
- Not "space" (space is the static picture; the Medium is the dynamic causal structure)

## 2. Mathematical Roles the Medium Must Satisfy

### 2a. Causal Structure
A Lorentzian manifold (M, g) where the metric g defines light cones — the partition between what can and cannot causally influence what. In Special Relativity, this is Minkowski space. In General Relativity, it is a dynamic metric that evolves with energy-momentum content.

**Required:** The Medium must have a metric structure. No preferred frame (Lorentz invariance) unless a physical condensate breaks it with the breaking being testable.

### 2b. State Space
A Hilbert space H (or Fock space in QFT) over which the Medium's state is defined. The vacuum state |0⟩ is the Medium's ground configuration — not empty, but the lowest-energy coherent configuration.

### 2c. Dynamics
A Lagrangian L (or Hamiltonian H) that encodes the rules of change. The propagator G(x,y) = ⟨0|T{φ(x)φ(y)}|0⟩ is the Medium's response function — how a disturbance at x propagates to y, encoding causal velocity.

### 2d. Coherence Conditions
The stability structure of the dynamical equations. Modes satisfying coherence conditions persist (particles). Modes that do not, disperse (radiation, thermalization). The particle spectrum is the set of stable modes — poles of the propagator.

### 2e. Symmetry Structure
The symmetries the Medium respects. These determine which modes exist and how they interact:
- Lorentz symmetry → particles have spin, rest mass is defined
- U(1) gauge symmetry → electromagnetism, photon is massless
- SU(2)×U(1) → electroweak, W/Z bosons acquire mass via Higgs
- SU(3) → quantum chromodynamics, quark confinement

**ℤ₃ claim (PF-specific — PREDICTED, not DERIVED):** If ℤ₃ topology is a symmetry of the Medium's vacuum state, exactly three stable winding configurations exist — three generations. This is a hypothesis about the Medium's topology, not a requirement of the definition.

## 4. How Entanglement Fits Without FTL Signaling

Two entangled particles are not two separate systems with a mysterious connection. They are one extended coherent mode of the Medium — one quantum state |ψ_AB⟩ that cannot be written as |ψ_A⟩ ⊗ |ψ_B⟩.

The state was established when the particles were created together (locally, at a source event). It has been maintained through coherence — the Medium holding the pattern across space.

When particle A is measured: the global state is projected locally at A. The constraint on B is not a signal traveling from A to B. It is the global state, already defined, expressing its global character at B's location. No new causal influence propagates from A to B.

**The no-signaling proof:** The outcomes at B are random from B's perspective alone. Only when results from A and B are compared (via classical communication at speed ≤ c) does the correlation become visible. The correlation is non-local. The information is not.

**In Medium language:** The Medium can hold non-separable coherence states. This is a fact about its Hilbert space structure, not a violation of Axiom 2.

## 5. What Would Falsify This Definition

The definition of the Medium fails — requiring fundamental revision — if:

**F-1: Controllable FTL signaling is demonstrated.**
If entanglement or any mechanism can transmit an arbitrary message faster than c, Axiom 2 fails and the causal role of the definition collapses.

**F-2: Lorentz invariance is violated with a preferred frame at accessible energies.**
If experiments find systematic violations implying an absolute rest frame for the Medium, the definition must include that frame — making the Medium closer to ether, with all observational constraints.
*Current status: no violation found to beyond Planck energy for linear effects (GRB 221009A, 2024).*

**F-3: A fourth generation of stable fermions is confirmed.**
If a stable fourth generation exists, the ℤ₃ claim about three generations from Medium topology is falsified. The Medium definition itself survives; the ℤ₃ claim does not.

**F-4: No mathematical formulation can reproduce the particle spectrum from Medium properties.**
If after serious attempt, no consistent structure (metric + fields + vacuum + coherence conditions) can reproduce the particle spectrum, the framework is incomplete.

**F-5: A physical entity is found that is not a mode of any field.**
If something exists that cannot be described as a field excitation in any QFT extension, the "modes of the Medium" picture is fundamentally broken.
