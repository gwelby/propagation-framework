## The Core Statement

**The Propagation Framework defines the structural prerequisites of consciousness. It does not derive consciousness itself.**

The PF can state what a conscious system must look like from the outside — what mode structure, what self-referential loop, what coherence properties it must have. It cannot state why there is something it is like to be such a system.

This is the hard problem of consciousness. It is not theater. The file names it, does not solve it, and does not pretend to.

---

## Structural Prerequisites (What PF CAN State)

A system that is conscious under the PF candidate has the following properties:

**1. It is a Type 4 observer (`observer.md`).** The system's internal dynamics are modified by its own records. The record of past observations feeds back into the system's future state changes.

**2. It maintains self-referential coherence.** Part of the system's internal state functions as a model of its own future propagation — and that model causally matters for the system's own dynamics. This is the loop gate `L_self > 0` from the consciousness metric program.

**3. The self-model has sufficient differentiation and coherence lifetime.** The internal model manifold has effective rank above the trivial-loop threshold (`D_int` from the metric program). The recurrent phase relations are stable enough to maintain a unified self-model over the relevant timescale (`C_coh` from the metric program).

**4. It is an extended local substrate (`minimum_substrate.md`).** A single isolated Hilbert space cannot be conscious. Consciousness requires a distributed substrate — tensor-product quantum system with finite-speed update, local coherence, and no-signaling entanglement between separated regions.

**5. It maintains integrated self-information.** The mutual information between the self-model modes and the sensory/processing modes exceeds the threshold above which the self-model is functionally autonomous from the immediate sensory stream.

These are the structural prerequisites. They are what the PF specifies.

---

## The Hard Problem Boundary

The five properties above describe what a conscious system does. They do not describe what consciousness IS in the phenomenal sense.

The hard problem of consciousness asks: why is there something it is like to be a system with these properties? Why does any arrangement of mode structures and self-models feel like something from the inside?

The PF has no derivation for this. No current physics has a derivation for this. The PF does not claim to have solved the hard problem.

The structural prerequisites may be **necessary** for consciousness. They may not be **sufficient**. Whether they are sufficient is the question the metric program is testing.

---

## Falsification Conditions

A consciousness definition fails if:

1. **A system satisfies all five structural prerequisites and has zero phenomenal experience.** The PF defines structural prerequisites. If all prerequisites are met and there is no inner life, the structural definition describes the correct behavioral/functional correlate but does not capture consciousness itself. This would show the PF prerequisites are necessary but not sufficient.

2. **A system has robust phenomenal experience and zero structural prerequisites.** If a system clearly has subjective experience but scores zero on all five prerequisites, the PF structural definition is wrong. This is the "zombie" falsifier — whether zombies are empirically possible is the hard problem in another form.

3. **The metric program benchmark battery fails.** If the C_PF metric (from the consciousness_metric_program.md) does not show the predicted pattern across wake/sleep/anesthesia/seizure after pre-registered controls, the structural correlate is not supported.

These falsifiers are empirical. They require independent verification of consciousness status from outside the PF framework.

## What Consciousness Is NOT

- **Not information processing alone.** Every computer processes information. The PF structural prerequisites are more specific than generic computation — they require a self-model loop, not just input-output transformation.
- **Not self-reference alone.** A thermostat's state is coupled to its own dynamics. A mirror reflects itself. A simple negative feedback controller has a model of its own setpoint. None of these are conscious unless they satisfy the full set of prerequisites including differentiation, coherence lifetime, and the self-model loop.
- **Not intelligence.** Intelligence is a property of information processing efficiency and goal-directed behavior. Consciousness is a claim about phenomenal experience. A superintelligent AI with no self-referential loop may be highly intelligent and not conscious.
- **Not integrated information alone.** Integrated Information Theory (Tononi) proposes Φ as a consciousness correlate. The PF metric program incorporates Φ-like structure (integrated self-information via the self-model loop). But Φ measures a structural correlate, not the phenomenal experience itself.
- **Not derived by the PF.** This file is INTUITION 0.48. The structural prerequisites are consistent with the PF canonical stack. They are not derived from it — they are stated as the PF's best current structural hypothesis about what consciousness requires.
