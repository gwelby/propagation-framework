## The Definition

**The Medium is the minimal causal-coherence structure required for physical propagation: a state space with finite-speed causal evolution, stable coherent modes, gradients that alter propagation paths, and quantization conditions selecting which patterns persist.**

It is defined by these roles, not by a claimed substance.

Short form:

> The Medium is the rule-structure that lets distinguishable change propagate, cohere, curve, and become stable modes.

## For the General Reader

You have been taught to look at the universe backwards. When you look at the night sky, you see stars sitting inside an immense, empty container called space, ticking forward on a clock called time. But space is not an empty container, time is not a universal clock, and matter is not made of tiny, indestructible pebbles. At the absolute bottom of reality, there are no "things." There is only the capacity for change to occur, and the strict mathematical constraints governing how fast and how stably that change can ripple outward. We call this capacity the Medium. You are not an object sitting inside the Medium; you are a complex, beautifully stable ripple propagating through it.

## The Smallest Dot

**Human question:** *What is the smallest dot made of?*

**Wrong answer:** Tiny solid objects — pebbles, strings, loops.

**PF answer:** The smallest dot is not a noun. It is a verb. It is an event.

The smallest distinguishable change that the rules of reality allow to propagate. What we call "matter" is what happens when a change gets trapped in a stable, repeating, resonant loop. You are never looking at a solid object. You are looking at a beautifully sustained echo in the Medium.

## Entanglement Without FTL

Two entangled particles are one extended coherent mode of the Medium — one quantum state |ψ_AB⟩ that cannot be written as |ψ_A⟩ ⊗ |ψ_B⟩.

The state was established when they were created together, locally, at a source event. It has been maintained through coherence. When one is measured, the global state is projected at that location. The constraint on the other particle is not a signal traveling between them — it is the global state, already defined, expressing its global character.

**The outcomes at each particle are random locally.** The correlation only becomes visible when both results are compared via classical communication (speed ≤ c). The correlation is non-local. The information is not.

The Medium supports non-separable coherence states. This is a fact about its Hilbert space structure, not a violation of Axiom 2.

## Velocity Discipline

Apparent superluminal effects exist and are real. They do not violate Axiom 2:

| Effect | True? | Why it doesn't break Axiom 2 |
|--------|-------|------------------------------|
| Phase velocity > c in media | True | Phase carries no information; front velocity remains causal |
| Group velocity > c in anomalous dispersion | True | Apparent effect; signal/front velocity does not exceed c |
| Evanescent tunneling | True | Apparent FTL; no controlled information transfer |
| Cherenkov radiation | True | Particle exceeds c/n (medium phase velocity), not vacuum c |

**The principle:** *front velocity* and *controllable causal influence* are bounded by the Medium's causal velocity. Phase and some group-velocity artifacts are not.
