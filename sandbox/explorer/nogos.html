Why document failures?

A theory that knows what it cannot do is more valuable than one that only knows what it can. Every NO-GO entry here represents a genuine attempt — a route that seemed promising, was pursued seriously, and was closed by a specific technical objection. The objections are real. They are on record.

How to read a NO-GO

Each entry has three parts: the approach (what was tried), the failure mode (exactly where and why the chain broke), and the lesson (what this rules out and what it suggests instead). Do not re-attempt the approach without reading the source document.
