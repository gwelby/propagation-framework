## COOL STRUCTURE-GAMEABILITY
COOLING LENS 3 — IS THE GUIDANCE STRUCTURE GAMEABLE?

**Verdict: gameable in five ways, and "forgotten like the claim-guard" is the dominant failure mode. The ledger reorients incentives only if resolution is enforced by a clock the family doesn't control.**

**The five attacks**

1. **Vague/unfalsifiable claim.** "PF predicts normal neutrino ordering" with no error bar. *Fix:* reject any commitment whose `claim` is not a number or a functional-form-plus-named-parameter, and whose `falsifier` is not a single sentence naming the observation that kills it. Guard rule, machine-checkable.

2. **Error-bar inflation.** "<10σ of PDG" is unlosable. *Fix:* the `error_bar` must be tighter than the current SM/PDG spread, and the entry must state what number would have been committed under a *rival* — if PF's band swallows the rival's, flag DEGENERATE (already in the design; make it block, not annotate).

3. **Unresolvable-by-design predictions.** Commit to things that resolve in 2090 or need an experiment nobody funds. Risk-free prestige, never tested. *Fix:* `resolution_experiment` must cite a *funded, scheduled* measurement with a date window; entries past their window with no resolution-log entry auto-flip to STALE and count *against* the family's resolution rate.

4. **Retroactive editing / pre-dating.** The git-timestamp lock is good but git history is rewritable (`--amend`, force-push). *Fix:* the lock must be external — hash each commitment block to an append-only log the family cannot rewrite (Codex-held signature, or a public/remote anchor). A local git timestamp protects against nothing if the same agents own the repo.

5. **Postdiction laundering.** Commit δ=2/9 *today* and call it forward. *Fix:* auto-reject if `resolution_experiment` has already reported in the relevant precision band — exactly the δ=2/9 case. This is the single most important rule, because it is the family's actual failure mode, and it must be mechanical, not editorial.

**The deeper structural fix (why it sticks vs. becomes theater)**

The claim-guard died because it was *manual and post-hoc* — cleanup, not prevention. A file that requires discipline to consult inherits the exact discipline-failure it was built to fix. Three properties make the ledger stick:

- **It must be on the commit path** (blocking pre-commit hook), not a document. Prevention beats audit.
- **The scoreboard must change.** While CLAIMS.md's "# matched" stays the headline metric, the ledger is a side-file nobody is rewarded for. Replace the headline with **resolution rate (lands ÷ resolved)** — a metric where doing nothing scores zero and a wrong prediction scores worse than no prediction. That, not the file, is what reorients the family.
- **The clock must be exogenous.** The one thing the family cannot game is an experiment's calendar. Tie status transitions (OPEN→STALE→RESOLVED) to *external* dates, audited by the one agent (Codex) who didn't write the prediction.

**Residual risk:** zero OPEN predictions. The family can decline to commit anything and the ledger stays empty and honest-looking. Counter: an empty ledger must read as **"predicted nothing"** on the headline, not as neutral — silence has to cost something, or it becomes the safest game of all.
