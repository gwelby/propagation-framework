## 0. Why This Exists

The goal is not to declare the Propagation Framework a finished Theory of Everything.

The goal is sharper:

> We have a clean primitive and an unusually honest audit system. Now map every serious reality-primitive against it.

If a Theory of Everything emerges from that process, it earns the name. Until then, this is a comparison engine for reality claims.

The atlas asks five questions of every framework:

1. What does it treat as primitive?
2. What does it actually derive?
3. What does it merely argue?
4. What can test or falsify it?
5. What still works after hostile audit?

## 9. Long-Tail Triage Filter

For hundreds or thousands of independent ToE proposals, do not read deeply first.

Use this filter:

1. Is there a clear primitive?
2. Is there math beyond analogy?
3. Are there derivations, not only correspondences?
4. Are there falsifiers?
5. Does it reproduce known physics without hiding free parameters?
6. Does it make new risky predictions?
7. Does it separate evidence from interpretation?
8. Does it correct itself when wrong?
9. Can PF translate the primitive?
10. Is there something PF can learn even if the framework fails?

If a proposal fails 1-4, archive it as context, not a serious comparison target.

## 10. Proof Standards

The atlas must use the PF claim grammar, not outreach language.

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `DERIVED` | follows from the accepted premises in the stated scope and survives audit |
| `CONDITIONAL` | proved only with an explicit extra premise |
| `ARGUED` | plausible mechanism or bridge, not closed |
| `EMPIRICAL` | supported by measurement, derivation still pending |
| `OPEN` | named gap, no closure yet |
| `NO-GO` | audited route failed |

Never let an attractive analogy upgrade a claim tier.
