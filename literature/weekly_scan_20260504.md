## 1. Koide Formula + Mass Matrix

### 1.1 A rich structure of renormalization group flows for Higgs-like models in 4 dimensions
- **arXiv ID:** [2411.07476v4](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.07476) (updated 4 Apr 2026)
- **Authors:** André LeClair
- **Published:** 2024-11-12 (v4: 2026-04-04) | **Categories:** hep-th, hep-ph
- **Abstract:** Two coupled Higgs doublets under SU(2) produce cyclic RG flows. The Hamiltonian is pseudo-hermitian (real eigenvalues, negative-norm states) and unitary below pair-production threshold. Spontaneous symmetry breaking yields an infinite tower of VEVs satisfying "Russian Doll" scaling `v_n ~ e^{2nλ}`. The period λ is RG-invariant. The strongest phenomenological constraint on λ comes from the Koide formula: `λ ≈ π/2`. The model naturally yields exactly 3 RG cycles, interpreted as 3 families of quarks and leptons.
- **Relevance to PF work:** **HIGH** — cyclic/Russian-Doll scaling mirrors self-referential propagation structures. Koide as a constraint on RG period suggests mass relations are not accidental but emergent from coherent propagation dynamics (Axiom 3 territory). The pseudo-hermiticity / non-unitarity boundary is also interesting for PF’s stance on effective vs. fundamental descriptions.

## 2. Fisher Information Metric + Particle Physics

### 2.1 Naturalness and Fisher Information
- **arXiv ID:** [2603.01411v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.01411) (updated 1 May 2026)
- **Authors:** James Halverson, Thomas R. Harvey, Michael Nee
- **Published:** 2026-03-02 (v2: 2026-05-01) | **Categories:** hep-th, hep-ex, hep-ph
- **Abstract:** Proposes a new measure of fine-tuning based on information theory. To each point in parameter space a probability distribution over observables is assigned; divergence measures encode sensitivity and determine a Riemannian metric. By Chentsov’s theorem, the physically motivated metric is the Fisher information metric. A rescaled fine-tuning matrix `ℱ_ij` is derived, reproducing the Barbieri–Giudice criterion as a special case while generalizing to multiple correlated parameters. Illustrated on dimensional transmutation, the Wilson–Fisher fixed point, the hierarchy problem, and the electron Yukawa coupling.
- **Relevance to PF work:** **HIGH** — PF treats energy as frequency and propagation as fundamental; Fisher information provides a geometric measure of how observable structure (masses, couplings) responds to parameter changes. Could inform Axiom-3-style selection principles: if “natural” parameter-space directions correspond to high-Fisher directions, coherence thresholds might map onto information-geometry constraints.

### 2.2 Leggett–Garg Inequality Violations Bound Quantum Fisher Information
- **arXiv ID:** [2604.09772](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.09772)
- **Authors:** Nick Abboud, Yuntao Guan, Barry Bradlyn, Jorge Noronha
- **Published:** 2026-04-10 | **Categories:** quant-ph, cond-mat.stat-mech, hep-th
- **Abstract:** A violation of a Leggett–Garg inequality for bounded observables in stationary pure states and thermal states yields a rigorous lower bound on the quantum Fisher information. Temporal correlations of a single collective observable can serve as an experimentally accessible witness of many-body quantum coherence, without full state reconstruction.
- **Relevance to PF work:** **MEDIUM** — connects macroscopic temporal correlations (LGI violations) to microscopic quantum sensitivity (QFI). Relevant for PF’s coherence axiom because it suggests that temporal-correlation experiments (like the planned LGI/Muse protocols) can quantitatively bound the “coherence” a system carries.

### 2.3 Quantum Fisher Information Revealing Parameter Sensitivity in Long-Baseline Neutrino Experiments
- **arXiv ID:** [2602.05221](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.05221)
- **Authors:** Bhavna Yadav, Amir Subba, Yu Shi
- **Published:** 2026-02-05 | **Categories:** hep-ph, quant-ph
- **Abstract:** Uses quantum Fisher information to establish fundamental precision bounds on single-parameter estimation in three-flavor ν_μ → ν_e oscillations. δ_CP and θ_23 exhibit bimodal QFI profiles; Δm²₃₁ shows unimodal structure peaking at L/E ~ 1000–1200 km/GeV with F_Q ~ 3×10⁶.

## 3. Coherence + Quantum Field Theory

### 3.1 Coherent-state boundary conditions as the first-principles origin of background fields in QED
- **arXiv ID:** [2512.21122v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.21122) (updated 18 Feb 2026)
- **Authors:** Keita Seto
- **Published:** 2025-12-24 (v3: 2026-02-18) | **Categories:** physics.plasm-ph, hep-ph, physics.optics, quant-ph
- **Abstract:** QED with prescribed classical background fields is not a distinct theory but arises as a well-defined boundary-condition limit of full QED. The classical background field emerges from coherent-state boundary conditions on the quantized electromagnetic field. Operator-level proof is given; relaxing boundary conditions allows depletion/backreaction within the same framework. The apparent time dependence of background fields reflects the Schrödinger vs. Heisenberg picture choice.
- **Relevance to PF work:** **HIGH** — directly connects “coherence” (coherent states) to the emergence of effective classical fields. For PF, this is a concrete example of Axiom 3 in action: coherent propagation patterns (coherent states) produce stable macroscopic structure (background fields). The picture-dependence observation is also philosophically aligned with PF’s view that time is an internal feature of propagation, not an external parameter.

### 4.1 Higher Representations and Quark Confinement
- **arXiv ID:** [2501.09069v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.09069) (updated 17 Feb 2025)
- **Authors:** Finn Gagliano, Andrea Grigoletto, Kantaro Ohmori
- **Published:** 2025-01-15 (v2: 2025-02-17) | **Categories:** hep-th, cond-mat.str-el, math-ph, math.QA
- **Abstract:** In the absence of 1-form symmetries, confinement is not a well-defined phase property. At zero temperature and fixed quark mass, the confined and adjoint Higgs phases of scalar QCD are distinguished through spectral organization from the baryon-symmetry perspective, using higher-categorical representation theory. Confined phase permits only integer baryon charges; Higgs phase shows coexistence of bare quarks and center vortices with non-trivial Aharonov–Bohm effect.
- **Relevance to PF work:** **HIGH** — higher-categorical / representation-theoretic classification of confinement phases is structurally adjacent to PF’s interest in discrete symmetry selectors (Z₃, Z₆, circulant matrices). ### 4.2 Quark confinement in presence of both chromoelectric and chromomagnetic fields and the structure of spacetime
- **arXiv ID:** [2508.04906](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.04906)
- **Authors:** Abdellah Touati, Soufiane Boukhalfa
- **Published:** 2025-08-06 | **Categories:** hep-ph, hep-th
- **Abstract:** Strong interaction in curved spacetime is investigated with a new non-abelian SU(3) gauge potential containing both chromoelectric and chromomagnetic fields (including a magnetic-monopole-like term) plus a radial Coulomb-like component. A spacetime metric induced by the Yang–Mills field is derived by solving Einstein’s equations in the limit where SU(3) reduces to an embedded SU(2) plus a dynamical U(1) monopole. The Schwarzschild radius of the strong interaction corresponds approximately to hadron size; confinement is interpreted as a geometric property of spacetime without introducing any confinement potential. Energy spectra reproduce hadron masses accurately.
- **Relevance to PF work:** **HIGH** — “confinement as geometry” is strongly aligned with PF’s Axiom 2 (finite causal velocity / propagation geometry). If quark binding emerges from spacetime structure rather than an inserted potential, this supports PF’s strategy of deriving forces from propagation geometry rather than positing them. The SU(3)→SU(2)×U(1) embedding may also connect to PF’s open T2 order-parameter and Fermi-point questions.

### 4.3 Measuring spin correlation between quarks during QCD confinement
- **arXiv ID:** [2506.05499v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.05499) (updated 9 Feb 2026)
- **Authors:** The STAR Collaboration
- **Published:** 2025-06-05 (v2: 2026-02-09) | **Categories:** hep-ex, hep-ph, hep-th, nucl-ex, nucl-th
- **Abstract:** Reports evidence of spin correlations in ΛΛ̄ hyperon pairs inherited from spin-correlated strange quark-antiquark virtual pairs. STAR experiment at RHIC reveals a relative polarization signal of (18 ± 4)% linking virtual spin-correlated quark pairs from the QCD vacuum to final-state hadrons. Provides an empirical anchor for PF’s claim that coherence is the operative principle in both bound-state formation and decoherence/dissipation.
