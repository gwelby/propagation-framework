**Title:** Koide's Z₃-symmetric parametrization, quark masses and mixings
**Authors:** Piotr Zenczykowski (Institute of Nuclear Physics, Polish Academy of Sciences)
**arXiv:** [1301.4143](https://arxiv.org/abs/1301.4143) | Published: 2013-01-17
**Categories:** hep-ph

**Abstract excerpt:**
> The sets of charged-lepton (L) and quark (D,U) masses may be parametrized in a Z₃-symmetric language appropriate for the discussion of Koide's formula.
