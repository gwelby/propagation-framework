## The Approach

Instead of asking "can PF derive X from axioms?", ask: **"This is measured. Does it fit PF's structure? Does PF predict something the Standard Model doesn't? Where are the gaps?"**

Each measurement gets an alignment score:
- 🟢 **FITS** — PF has a structural explanation or exact identity
- 🟡 **ALIGNS** — PF's structure is consistent with the measurement but doesn't uniquely predict it
- 🟠 **GAP** — PF has threads but no account yet
- 🔴 **SILENT** — PF has nothing on this
