## 1. Why compare these two

Mainstream cosmology explains cosmic acceleration with **dark energy**, most commonly modeled as a cosmological constant Λ in Einstein's field equations. The Propagation Framework (PF) is an internal research program that asks whether spacetime, fields, and particles can be organized from propagation itself. Both frameworks must face the same data: the universe is expanding faster than matter alone can explain, and the energy density associated with this acceleration is extraordinarily small compared to naive quantum-field-theory estimates.

This draft compares what each framework can honestly say today.

## 2. Four viewpoints

### Age 5 — "What is the dark stuff that pushes the universe apart?"

**Mainstream:** Scientists don't know what dark energy is. They call it "dark" because it doesn't emit light, and "energy" because it makes space expand faster. The simplest model is a constant built into space itself.

**PF:** We also don't know what dark energy is. Our framework suggests that the vacuum of space might not be empty — it might be a medium that carries propagation. But we have not yet derived why the universe accelerates, and we do not have a specific dark-energy particle or field.

**Common ground:** Both frameworks treat the observational fact (cosmic acceleration) as real; both treat the underlying mechanism as open.

### Student — "Doesn't ΛCDM just work? Why do we need another framework?"

**Mainstream:** ΛCDM fits an enormous amount of data with six parameters. It does not explain why Λ has the value it has; the naive QFT prediction for vacuum energy is ~10¹²⁰ times larger. This is the cosmological constant problem.

**PF:** PF does not replace ΛCDM. It is an internal research program that tries to derive the structure of the medium from propagation. The honest current result is that PF can derive the holographic dilution factor N = (λ_c/l_P)² that recovers Newton's G to better than 1%, and it can rule out several wrong ideas (e.g., G varying with the Hubble radius, Dirac's Large Numbers Hypothesis). It does not yet explain Λ.

**Common ground:** Both frameworks leave the cosmological constant problem unsolved. PF's contribution is a narrower set of derived constraints, not a solution.

### PhD — "What does PF actually derive, and what is still open?"

**Mainstream (ΛCDM):**
- The Friedmann equation with Λ reproduces the observed expansion history.
- The value of Λ is an input, not an output.
- The cosmological constant problem (why Λ is so small) is unsolved.

**PF (internal research):**
- **Derived / strongly constrained:**
  - The holographic dilution factor N = (λ_c/l_P)² ≈ 5 × 10³³ is the correct scale for recovering G; the Hubble-radius alternative N = (R_H/l_P)² gives G wrong by ~10⁸⁹ (DERIVED 0.95 / ruled out 0.99).
  - G is constant; N is fixed because λ_c and l_P are constants (consistent with Ġ/G < 10⁻¹² yr⁻¹) (EMPIRICAL 0.97).
  - Dirac's Large Numbers Hypothesis, which predicts Ġ/G ~ −7 × 10⁻¹¹ yr⁻¹, is experimentally ruled out by Lunar Laser Ranging at >50σ (EMPIRICAL 0.99).
- **Speculative / open:**
  - Whether PF axioms can derive Λ or a dark-energy mechanism (confidence 0.05–0.30).
  - Whether Verlinde's entropic/elastic dark-energy mechanism can be derived from PF axioms (ARGUED 0.30).
  - The UV cutoff at λ_c reduces the vacuum-energy mismatch from 10¹²⁰ to ~10⁵⁸, but this is still not a solution (CONDITIONAL / open).

**Common ground:** Both frameworks have precise observational successes and shared open problems. PF does not currently solve the cosmological constant problem.

### Master — "Where are the real boundaries?"

**Mainstream boundary:** ΛCDM is a phenomenological model. It does not claim to derive Λ from first principles. Competing frameworks (quintessence, modified gravity, emergent gravity) remain viable because the data do not uniquely select a mechanism.

**PF boundary:** PF is a research program, not established physics. The honest claims are:
- Axioms 1–3 plus the holographic counting yield a derivation of G and a fixed N (DERIVED 0.95 within the stated static/stationary gravity scope).
- The same axioms do not yet derive Λ, the dark energy equation of state w(z), or a cosmic expansion history.
- The coincidence Λ ~ H₀²/c² is noted but not derived; it is the same coincidence-of-scales observation made by Verlinde, Padmanabhan, and others.

**Critical difference:** ΛCDM takes Λ as a parameter. PF tries to derive the medium and is currently honest about not having derived Λ. The frameworks are not in contradiction; they operate at different stages of explanation.

## 4. What PF does NOT claim

- PF does not derive the dark energy density, Λ, or w(z).
- PF does not replace ΛCDM or claim the cosmological constant problem is solved.
- PF does not predict a specific dark-energy particle or field.
- PF does not claim G varies with cosmic time; the framework predicts G is constant.
- PF does not claim the Hubble radius is the holographic screen for gravity; it predicts λ_c is.

## 5. What would change the picture

For PF:
- A derivation of Λ from Axioms 1–3 (or a minimal explicit premise) would be a major breakthrough.
- A proof that the PF axioms force Λ = 0 at tree level, with radiative corrections matching the observed value, would solve the problem.
- A derivation of Verlinde's entropic/elastic dark-energy mechanism from PF axioms would make the framework cosmologically predictive.

For mainstream:
- A measurement of w(z) ≠ −1 would rule out a true cosmological constant and favor dynamical dark energy.
- A precision measurement of Ġ/G consistent with Dirac's hypothesis would challenge both PF and ΛCDM.
