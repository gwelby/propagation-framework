## 1. Why compare these three

Mainstream physics has two leading research programs that try to reconcile gravity with quantum mechanics:

- **Loop Quantum Gravity (LQG):** quantizes spacetime geometry itself using spin networks and spin foams. No extra dimensions, no new particles beyond the Standard Model, no unification of forces.
- **String Theory / M-Theory:** posits that the fundamental objects are one-dimensional strings (or higher-dimensional branes). Requires extra spatial dimensions (typically 10 or 11) and predicts a unified framework of all forces, but has not yet produced a unique, testable prediction for our universe.

The Propagation Framework (PF) is an internal research program that asks whether spacetime, fields, and particles can be organized from propagation itself. It is not a quantum gravity theory in the same sense, but it sits in the same conceptual space: it tries to derive structure from something more primitive than spacetime.

This draft compares what each framework can honestly say today.

## 2. Four viewpoints

### Age 5 — "What is the smallest thing in the universe?"

**LQG:** Space itself might be made of tiny loops. It is not smooth all the way down; it has a smallest possible size, like pixels on a screen.

**String Theory:** The smallest things are tiny vibrating strings. Different vibrations make different particles. The strings need extra dimensions we can't see.

**PF:** We are trying to find out if everything can be explained by things moving and affecting each other. We don't know yet if that gives us tiny loops, strings, or something else. It is early research, not established physics.

**Common ground:** All three frameworks try to go deeper than the current Standard Model. None of them is proven to be the right answer.

### Student — "Why do we need any of these frameworks?"

**Mainstream:** General relativity and quantum mechanics work extremely well in their own domains, but they break down in extreme conditions (black holes, the Big Bang). We need a theory that handles both. LQG and string theory are the two biggest research programs.

**PF:** PF does not replace LQG or string theory. It is a different starting point: instead of quantizing gravity or adding strings, PF asks if spacetime and quantum fields can be derived from propagation axioms. The honest current result is that PF can derive gravity as optical geometry for null geodesics in static/stationary spacetimes, a kernel-only topological obstruction, and a premise-bounded Koide identity. It does not yet quantize spacetime, predict extra dimensions, or derive quantum gravity.

**Common ground:** All three are incomplete. LQG has mathematical consistency but struggles to recover classical GR and make predictions. String theory has rich mathematics but no unique vacuum/prediction. PF has narrow derived results but no quantum gravity story yet.

### PhD — "What does each framework actually derive?"

**LQG:**
- Derives area and volume spectra from spin networks (quantized geometry).
- Predicts a minimum area scale and a finite black-hole entropy proportional to horizon area (up to the Immirzi ambiguity).
- Has not yet recovered classical general relativity in a continuum limit or made unique testable predictions.

**String Theory:**
- Requires 10 or 11 dimensions for mathematical consistency.
- Contains graviton-like states automatically; includes gauge interactions (so it can unify forces).
- The landscape problem: ~10^500 possible vacua; no unique prediction for particle masses, constants, or dimensionality of our universe.
- No direct experimental confirmation.

**PF (internal research):**
- **Derived / strongly constrained:**
  - Gravity as optical geometry for null geodesics in static/stationary spacetimes (DERIVED 0.95).
  - A kernel-only topological obstruction in Lean 4 (`TopologicalWeights.lean`, DERIVED 0.95 algebraic scope).
  - A premise-bounded geometric identity associated with the Koide ratio (exact given equal-amplitude premise; physical selection open).
  - Constant G and fixed holographic dilution scale N = (λ_c/l_P)², with Dirac's varying-G hypothesis ruled out (EMPIRICAL / DERIVED 0.97).
- **Not derived / open:**
  - Quantum gravity (spacetime quantization, graviton, black-hole entropy from first principles).
  - Extra dimensions.
  - String-like objects or branes.
  - A unique vacuum or particle spectrum.

**Common ground:** All three frameworks have mathematical successes and shared open problems. PF's honest boundary is narrower: it does not currently claim to be a quantum gravity theory.

### Master — "Where are the real boundaries?"

**LQG boundary:** It is a background-independent quantization of geometry. It does not require extra dimensions or unification, but it also does not explain why the Standard Model has the particles and forces it does.

**String Theory boundary:** It is a unified theory of quantum gravity and forces, but it requires accepting extra dimensions and a vast landscape of possible universes. It has not yet made a unique, falsifiable prediction for our universe.

**PF boundary:** PF is a research program, not established physics. It tries to derive the structure of the medium from propagation axioms. The honest claims are narrow and bounded:
- It derives certain geometric/optical structures from propagation axioms.
- It does not derive quantum gravity, extra dimensions, strings, or a unique particle spectrum.
- It is an internal research program; no public-ready quantum gravity claim exists.

**Critical difference:** LQG and string theory are attempts to quantize gravity or unify forces. PF is an attempt to derive spacetime and fields from propagation. The frameworks are not in direct competition; they ask different questions at different stages of explanation.

- PF does not quantize spacetime.
- PF does not predict extra dimensions.

- PF does not predict string-like objects or branes.

- A derivation of a quantum gravity effect (e.g., black-hole entropy, graviton, or Planck-scale discreteness) from Axioms 1–3 would be a major breakthrough.
- A proof that the propagation axioms force a unique dimensionality or particle spectrum would make PF competitive with string theory's landscape problem.
- A successful PRED-002 (neutrino Koide non-universality) would show PF can make discriminating predictions without needing quantum gravity first.

For LQG / string theory:
- A confirmed prediction of quantized area, Lorentz-invariance violation at Planck scale, or a unique stringy signature would be breakthrough evidence.
- A derivation of the Standard Model from string compactification would solve the landscape problem.
