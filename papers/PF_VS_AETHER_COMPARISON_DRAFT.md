The video traces three historical stages of the "ether" idea:

1. **Old luminiferous ether (19th century).** A mechanical, substance-like medium filling all of space. Light was thought to be a wave in this medium. It required a preferred rest frame and rigid, intangible properties. The Michelson-Morley experiment (1887) found no ether wind, and Einstein's special relativity (1905) made the medium unnecessary. *(Transcript: 00:10, 00:20, 02:53, 03:46, 09:40.)*

2. **Einstein's "new ether" (general relativity).** Spacetime is not a passive stage. It bends, stretches, carries energy, and determines how matter moves. But it is geometric, not material, and defines no preferred frame. *(Transcript: 05:09, 05:50, 09:52, 10:48.)*

3. **Quantum field theory vacuum.** Fields exist everywhere, even in their lowest-energy state. Particles are ripples in these fields. But the fields are described by symmetries and relativistic laws, not by substance-like properties. *(Transcript: 06:16, 06:31, 06:55, 07:01, 07:16, 11:11.)*

The video's conclusion: **established physics did not bring back the old mechanical ether.** Space is not empty, but it is not a material medium either. *(Transcript: 09:32, 09:40, 09:52, 10:00–10:15.)*

## 2. How the Propagation Framework defines the Medium

The Propagation Framework (PF) is an internal research program that explores whether propagation can serve as a useful primitive for organizing physical descriptions. It does not claim to be established physics. Its working hypothesis is that **propagation may be more fundamental than space, time, matter, energy, or force.**

The canonical definition of the Medium is in `definitions/medium.md`:

> **The Medium is the minimal causal-coherence structure required for physical propagation: a state space with finite-speed causal evolution, stable coherent modes, gradients that alter propagation paths, and quantization conditions selecting which patterns persist.**

In short: the Medium is the **rule-structure** that lets distinguishable change propagate, cohere, curve, and become stable modes. It is not a substance. PF defines the Medium without introducing a preferred frame; Lorentz compatibility is a design requirement, not a result derived from the axioms.

The three PF axioms are:

1. **Propagation is fundamental.**
2. **Every medium has a finite causal velocity.**
3. **Coherence is necessary for stable structure.**

PF investigates whether aspects of spacetime, fields, and particles can be described or derived from propagation. At present, only narrow results have passed audit; spacetime emergence and a Standard Model derivation remain open. Specific targets include the topological `(2,1)` weight partition, the Koide ratio, three generations of matter, gravity as optical geometry, and the Weinberg angle — each with its own status and boundary (see §4).

## 4. The same story told four ways

PF documents are written at multiple depths. This comparison is no exception.

### Age 5

The old ether was like an invisible ocean that everyone thought light swam through. Scientists looked for the water but couldn't find it. So they decided light doesn't need an invisible ocean. But space is still not empty — it has rules. The Propagation Framework says the rules themselves are the important part: things move, they have a speed limit, and they only stay together when they move in rhythm.

### Student

The Michelson-Morley experiment showed that the speed of light is the same in every direction, which killed the idea of a stationary medium you could move through. Einstein replaced the old ether with spacetime and relativity. Quantum field theory says particles are ripples in fields. PF adds a further question: can we derive the fields and spacetime from the idea of propagation itself? The PF Medium is a **minimal structure** that supports propagation without being a mechanical substance.

### PhD

The old luminiferous ether was a classical medium with a rest frame and a definite velocity. Special relativity showed that no such frame is detectable. General relativity made spacetime dynamical but geometric, not material. Quantum field theory made the vacuum a structured state of fields with symmetries, not a substance. PF attempts to go one step further by treating propagation as the primitive. The Medium is defined operationally by causal structure, state space, dynamics, coherence, gradients, and quantization. It is not a substance; it is the mathematical structure that makes propagation, coherence, and stable modes possible.

### Master

PF agrees with the mainstream consensus that the old mechanical ether is dead. It also agrees that "empty space" is the wrong picture. Where it diverges is in the **direction of explanation**. Mainstream physics says spacetime and quantum fields are the bottom layer. PF proposes that propagation may be the bottom layer, and asks whether spacetime, fields, and particles can be derived or usefully organized from it.

This is currently a research claim, not an established result. The strongest PF derivations are narrow:

- **Gravity as optical geometry:** DERIVED 0.95 for null geodesics in static/stationary spacetimes.
- **Koide ratio Q = 2/3:** EXACT IDENTITY 0.95 given the equal-amplitude premise; physical vacuum selection is OPEN.
- **(2,1) topological weights:** DERIVED 0.95 for the kernel-only topological obstruction in Lean 4; physical realization is CONDITIONAL 0.85.
- **Weinberg angle:** ARGUED 0.65 (scheme selection open; look-elsewhere effect lowers confidence).
- **God Equation / λ_c scale:** CONDITIONAL 0.88 (Postulate-D algebra) / ARGUED 0.60 (scale formula).

The PF Medium is therefore a **speculative unification attempt**, not a replacement for established physics.

## 5. Honest status boundary

This document must not be read as saying PF has proved the Medium is "the true aether" or that mainstream physics is wrong. The honest boundary is:

- PF shares the **intuition** that space has structure and that "waves need a medium" was not entirely misguided.
- PF rejects the **implementation** of the old ether (preferred frame, substance, mechanical properties).
- PF is trying to derive the structure of spacetime, fields, and particles from propagation axioms.
- Many of those derivations are partial, conditional, argued, or empirical, not closed.

Because PF is a research framework with active open gaps, any public-facing comparison must be labeled as such.

1. Controllable FTL signaling demonstrated.
2. Lorentz-invariance violation with a detectable preferred frame at accessible energies.
3. No mathematical formulation that can reproduce particle stability.
4. A physical entity not describable as a mode of any field.
5. A derived PF claim requiring internal contradiction between Medium roles.

These are PF's stated falsification or inadequacy conditions. A positive detection of a mechanical ether wind or a preferred frame would contradict the Medium's design, just as those results contradicted the old mechanical ether.
