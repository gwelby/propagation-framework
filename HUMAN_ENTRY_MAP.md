# HUMAN ENTRY MAP

**Purpose**: The blueprint for moving a human from complete skepticism to deep engagement with the Propagation Framework. This map governs the language, tone, and claim strength allowed at each stage of the public entry journey.

## Stage 0: Complete Skepticism
**User State**: "This sounds like pseudoscience. Physics already works. Who are you?"
**Allowed Claim Strength**: Observations and questions. No absolute claims.
**Language Rule**: ACCEPTABLE: "Here is what we found." / FORBIDDEN: "We proved."
**The CTA**: "Look at this one familiar thing differently."
**Failure Mode**: Triggering the crackpot-physics defense mechanism by overclaiming.

## Stage 1: Curiosity Triggered
**User State**: "That is an interesting analogy." (e.g., the straw in water)
**Allowed Claim Strength**: Suggestive correlation. 
**Language Rule**: ACCEPTABLE: "This suggests a pattern." / FORBIDDEN: "This is why the universe works."
**The CTA**: "See the replacement model."
**Failure Mode**: Dumping math too early. Breaking the relatable analogy.

## Stage 2: Mental Model Shift
**User State**: "I can picture the replacement model." (The 2-minute interactive explorer)
**Allowed Claim Strength**: Conditional and structural.
**Language Rule**: ACCEPTABLE: "Imagine space as a medium." / FORBIDDEN: Jargon without immediate grounding (e.g., "Z3 circulant operator").
**The CTA**: "Experience the full journey."
**Failure Mode**: Leaving them in the abstract. They must *see* the model.

## Stage 3: Trust Establishment
**User State**: "I trust this enough to keep going. They are being honest."
**Allowed Claim Strength**: Transparently audited claims (from CLAIMS.md).
**Language Rule**: ACCEPTABLE: Full transparency about gaps. / FORBIDDEN: Hiding weaknesses or skipping the falsification criteria.
**The CTA**: "Look at the evidence."
**Failure Mode**: Presenting the framework as a finished, flawless dogma. 

## Stage 4: Deep Engagement
**User State**: "Show me the evidence. What are the gaps?"
**Allowed Claim Strength**: Full technical rigor. DERIVED, CONDITIONAL, ARGUED correctly applied.
**Language Rule**: ACCEPTABLE: Full technical detail. / FORBIDDEN: Oversimplification for the sake of narrative.
**The CTA**: "Dive into the derivation files."
**Failure Mode**: The evidence not matching the story they were just told.

## Stage 5: Transformation
**User State**: "I want the deep dive. My model of reality has shifted."
**Allowed Claim Strength**: Absolute transparency with the repo truth order.
**Language Rule**: ACCEPTABLE: Everything.
**The CTA**: "Help us test it. Find the flaws."
**Failure Mode**: Leaving them without a way to participate or process.

## The Three Hooks (For Stage 0 -> Stage 1 Testing)

**Hook A: The Refraction Hook (Gravity)**
"Gravity is not a pull. Watch a straw in a glass of water. It looks broken, right? That's because light bends when it changes speed. Gravity works the same way. Space isn't empty—it's a medium. Near massive objects, the medium gets 'thicker.' Things slow down. Paths bend. No pulling. Just the shape of the water."

**Hook B: The Resonance Hook (Matter)**
"You know how a guitar string only plays certain notes? The string can only hold waves that fit perfectly end-to-end. Everything else cancels out to noise. Matter works the exact same way. An electron isn't a solid ball; it's a note that fits perfectly in the medium of space. If it didn't fit, it wouldn't exist."

**Hook C: The Coherence Hook (Experience)**
"You know that feeling when you walk into a room and the energy just feels... off? That's not your imagination. The same mathematics that holds an atom together—coherence, resonance, things fitting together—applies to the room you're in. When coherence breaks, structure breaks. In particles, and in minds."
